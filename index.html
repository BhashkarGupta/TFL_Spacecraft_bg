TFL Spacecraft-bg

An interactive background and spacecraft flight cursor engine featuring high-voltage electric plasma networks, dual-mode tractor beam dynamics, hold-to-charge particle attraction, kinetic release shockwaves, cosmic nebula clouds, real-time banking orientation, dual ion exhaust trails, and adaptive performance fallback across both light and dark themes.

1. Electric Plasma Network

Interactive canvas nodes drift autonomously, generating dynamic high-voltage branched electric arcs and atmospheric super-arc filaments connecting neighboring charges.

2. Interactive Particle Interaction

Moving the spacecraft cursor near nodes automatically forms live glowing electrical tethers, drawing nearby particles into flight formation at cruising speeds.

3. Slingshot Acceleration Breakaway

When the spacecraft accelerates rapidly, tethered nodes experience breaking strain, flinging loose into the slipstream with visible kinetic spark discharges.

4. Flight Cursor & Dual Ion Exhaust

Hardware-level 1:1 cursor tracking with 360-degree directional vector banking, interactive element targeting lock, dual nozzle flame scaling, and persistent glowing particle trails.

5. Idle Autopilot Cruise

If user interaction ceases for 15 seconds (customizable), the spacecraft automatically launches into a gentle patrol cruise across the page, waking upon cursor input.

6. Adaptive Performance Engine

Built-in rolling FPS monitor automatically switches to lightweight zero-blur translucent styling if sustained frame rates drop below 30 FPS or when operating on mobile devices.

7. Hold-to-Charge & Kinetic Burst

Press and hold the mouse button to charge energy: nearby particles are gently drawn inward at a steady, majestic pace as the attraction field progressively expands across the screen to pull in distant particles. Attracted particles form live electric arcs with the spacecraft that intensify with vibrant fractal lightning and energetic sparks as they draw close. Releasing pulls particles slightly closer before violently repelling them away with radial shockwaves and scaled kinetic momentum.

8. Procedural Web Audio Sound FX

Zero external audio dependencies or network lag. Uses the browser's native Web Audio API to procedurally synthesize dynamic ion thruster velocity hums, hold-to-charge rising energy sweeps, explosive sub-bass kinetic shockwave thumps, high-velocity slingshot detachment snaps, and UI target-lock blips. Includes one-click audio muting and automatic localStorage persistence.
